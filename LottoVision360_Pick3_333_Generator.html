How the experimental method works

First calculate (entered number + 333) modulo 1000, preserving leading zeros. Generate eight variations of that three-digit seed by adding 5 modulo 10 to every possible subset of its digit positions. For each variation, make three circular rotations (ABC, BCA, CAB), yielding 24 straight entries before deduplication.

This is a new, transparent experimental formula—not a verified reconstruction of the generator in the video. Some entries may repeat, especially with repeated digits. The page reports distinct straight combinations. Lottery outcomes are random and this method has no established predictive advantage.
