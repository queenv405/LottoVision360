Scoring method

Uses the Excel-style Top 10 Filter scoring categories: due sum +30, due root +20, any listed digit pair +20, hot digits +5 each (warm +2.5), preferred digits in the matching position +5 each. Ties preserve the original 40-number generator order. The no-flips setting is an additional filter; turn it off to reproduce Excel's unfiltered ranking.

The seed is reference-only in the Excel pair generator. Manual inputs are editable snapshots. Dashboard refresh derives new inputs from the most recent bundled historical drawings, which can produce rankings different from the Excel snapshot. Scores are experimental and do not increase the odds of winning.
