How the experiment works

For each previous result ABCD, take its last digit D. Make eight 3-digit seed variants by adding 5 modulo 10 to every possible subset of the digits of 851. For each variant, create five ending digits: (D + 0), (D + 1), (D + 2), (D + 3), (D + 4), all modulo 10. Concatenate each variant with each ending digit to produce 40 straight numbers.

This is a transparent, provisional formula. It has not been verified as the exact formula used in earlier backtests or in the referenced video. Straight match means exact digit order; box match means the same digits in any order. These results are not predictions, and lottery outcomes are random.
